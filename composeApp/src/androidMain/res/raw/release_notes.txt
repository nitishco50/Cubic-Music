Cubic Music v2.0.0:
Highlights:
- Comments now support loading, posting, refresh, pagination, retry, and isolated failure handling.
- Downloads in HomeSongs load from local Media3 download data and metadata; offline loading ends on data or error instead of waiting indefinitely for network.
- Data Saver limits quality and feed loading on cellular, disables automatic downloads and Canvas there, and leaves Wi-Fi unaffected.
- Home Quick Picks and Casual show local content first, then enrich it with remote results.
- Home notices show cached content immediately while refreshing from JSON in the background.
- The queue highlights the current and next songs; the player's existing upcoming-song preview stays in sync.
- Queue state refreshes as songs change, are removed, or finish playing.
- Removing a queued song now targets that song even if the queue has changed.
- Automatic queue refill now respects its setting and the selected queue limit.
- Network refill controls for downloaded queues can collapse and reopen when tapped.
Improvements:
- The player sheet stays composed during show/hide animation for a smoother player-to-mini-player transition.
- Completed songs are removed from the active queue after natural playback, keeping Now Playing current.
- Queue refill also responds when the queue is shortened while playback is active.
- Failed refill requests no longer leave the queue stuck in a loading state.
- Fully downloaded queues remain offline unless Network refill is enabled.
